Who We Are

More Than a Fuel Supplier — A Solutions Partner

ASHCO Energy Ltd. is a reliable and highly dependable organization that supplies petroleum, oil, lubricants (POL) and allied products to clients across all provinces of Nigeria including neighboring countries. We hold CAC accreditation and a Department of Energy wholesale fuel license issued by the Standard Organisation of Nigeria (SON).

While ordinary wholesalers compete on price alone, we differentiate ourselves by providing unique solutions that directly address our customers' challenges — employing a consultative approach and involving clients in the process leading to a customised solution.
